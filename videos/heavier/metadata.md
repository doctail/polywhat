## Description
What if your mass doubled every single hour, and nothing could stop it?

Hour 3, your bed gives up. Hour 6, the floor. Hour 17, you sink through solid granite. Hour 31, you pull on everything around you like a tiny planet. Hour 77, you outweigh the entire Earth. And less than five days after you stepped on a scale, you're heavier than Sagittarius A*, the supermassive black hole at the center of the Milky Way.

All of it from one rule: 70 kg × 2, once an hour.

0:00 The scale
0:36 Sinking into the ground
2:00 A planet inside a planet
2:50 Falling to the core
4:05 Meanwhile, on the surface
5:30 You become a black hole
6:50 Heavier than Sagittarius A*
8:15 117 doublings

The numbers: mass = 70 kg × 2^hour, while your body stays the same size. Comparisons are approximate (the mass of the dinosaur-killing asteroid, for example, is only known to within a range).
